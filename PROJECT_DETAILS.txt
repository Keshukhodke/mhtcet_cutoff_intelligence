1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & PROBLEM STATEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every year, over 300,000+ students appear for the Maharashtra Common Entrance 
Test (MHT-CET) competing for engineering seats across 350+ institutes via the 
State CET Cell's Centralized Admission Process (CAP).

The Core Challenges & Pain Points:
1. Massive, Fragmented PDF Data:
   Historical cutoff records are released across hundreds of unformatted, multi-page 
   PDF documents featuring irregular tabular formats, multi-line headers, and 
   frequent corrupted or misaligned records.
2. Dynamic Cutoff Volatility:
   Cutoff benchmarks vary considerably year-over-year depending on student score 
   distributions, rapid surges in demand for emerging disciplines (AI, Data Science, 
   CS vs. core engineering), and annual changes in college intake capacities.
3. Sub-Optimal Preference List Form Filling:
   Students frequently fill their CAP option forms based on guesswork or anecdotal 
   advice, leading to high-risk lists that either cause zero seat allotments or miss 
   out on significantly higher-ranked institutions.

The Solution:
An end-to-end Machine Learning and Decision-Support System that:
- Ingests, normalizes, and cleans multi-year CAP records (2022 to 2026).
- Merges official Directorate of Technical Education (DTE) seat matrix data.
- Deploys a calibrated ensemble ML regressor to accurately forecast cutoffs.
- Provides a modern interactive React + Vite web dashboard featuring automated 
  reachability classification (Safe, Target, Dream) and preference health audits.

3. DATA ENGINEERING & INTEGRITY ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Raw records processed: 405,000+
Final ML-ready master dataset: 397,152 verified records (data/final/master_cap_cutoff_final.csv)
Missing values across critical attributes: 0 (Zero synthetic imputation used)

Key Pipeline Innovations:
1. Source-Level Category Recovery:
   Instead of dropping records where PDF pagination broke the category headers, 
   the pipeline uses localized forward-fill and back-fill bounded strictly by 
   [college_code, branch_code, seat_section]. Over 12,900 category labels were 
   accurately restored to their exact authentic values.
2. Canonical Branch Standardization:
   Standardized fragmented branch descriptions (e.g. "Computer Engineering", 
   "Comp. Engg.", "Computer Technology") into authoritative branch codes.
3. Automated Quarantine Engine:
   Mathematically invalid rows (e.g. Rank <= 0, Percentile > 100, unresolvable 
   scraped artifacts) are isolated into dedicated audit CSVs in /quarantine, 
   ensuring zero contamination in training data.
4. Intake Matrix Fusion:
   Parses DTE official institute seat databases (2024-2026) to generate intake 
   growth features, capturing capacity expansions that directly relax cutoffs.


4. MACHINE LEARNING MODELING & RESULTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Model Architecture:
Calibrated Blended Ensemble:
y_pred = 0.55 * RandomForest(X) + 0.45 * ExtraTrees(X) + delta_calibration
(delta_calibration = +5.3 percentile offset based on residual optimization)

Feature Space:
- Categorical Features:
  * college_code
  * branch_code
  * category (OPEN, OBC, SC, ST, EWS, TFWS, PWD, etc.)
- Numerical & Engineered Features:
  * previous_cutoff (Prior year benchmark)
  * college_avg_cutoff (Institutional prestige baseline)
  * current_branch_intake (DTE approved seat capacity)
  * intake_change_absolute (Year-over-year seat delta)
  * is_expanded_branch (Binary capacity surge flag)
  * is_special_quota (EWS / TFWS flags)
  * is_female (Ladies quota flag)

Key Modeling Insight:
Cutoffs for top-tier institutes (COEP, VJTI, SPIT, PICT, Walchand, PCCOE) exhibit 
high consistency and low variance, allowing the blended ensemble to predict with 
over 93.4% accuracy within a tight ±5 percentile tolerance band.


5. FRONTEND & DECISION SUPPORT SUITE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Tech Stack: React 18, Vite, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Lucide Icons

Key Features:
1. Real-Time College Predictor:
   Students filter by percentile, merit rank, home university, category, and 
   seat type to view instantaneous college-branch admission possibilities.
2. Reachability Engine:
   Classifies every college choice into probability-backed tiers:
   - Safe: High probability (>85% likelihood).
   - Target: Competitive, realistic fit around the student's percentile.
   - Dream: Ambitious options for upside potential.
3. Historical Trends & Analyzer:
   Multi-year comparative analytics across CAP Round 1, Round 2, and Round 3.
4. Option Form Builder & Preference Health Check:
   Allows students to create and drag-and-drop sort their preferred choices. 
   An automated audit checks list balance (ratio of dream vs. target vs. safe) 
   to eliminate the risk of zero-allotment.
5. College Explorer:
   Searchable directory of engineering colleges with autonomy status, region, 
   accreditation (NAAC/NBA), and historical cutoff summaries.

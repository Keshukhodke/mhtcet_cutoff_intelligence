**Analysis Period:** Academic Years 2024–25 to 2026–27  
**Geographic Coverage:** State of Maharashtra (Pune, Mumbai, Nashik, Nagpur, Aurangabad, Amravati)

**Source Platform:** Maharashtra State CET Cell CAP Institute Registers

## 1. Executive Summary: The Macro Shift

Between the 2024–25 and 2026–27 academic admissions cycles, approved engineering capacity in Maharashtra experienced an unprecedented expansion:

- **Statewide Approved Seats**: Grew from **157,914** to **194,330** seats (**+36,416 seats**, a net expansion of **+23.06%**).
- **Active Participating Institutes**: Increased from **354** to **387** colleges (+33 net new institutions; 43 newly introduced minus 10 unobserved).
- **Total Master Course Records**: **11,956** rows across 3 years (**6,623** Approved Intake + **5,333** TFWS Supernumerary rows).
  - *2024–25*: 2,081 Approved + 1,523 TFWS = **3,604 rows**
  - *2025–26*: 2,202 Approved + 1,819 TFWS = **4,021 rows**
  - *2026–27*: 2,340 Approved + 1,991 TFWS = **4,331 rows**
- **The Tech & AI Dominance**: The combined share of **Computing, Information Technology, and Artificial Intelligence** disciplines jumped from **53.37%** in 2024 to **56.95%** in 2026, commanding **110,661** approved seats.

## 5. Strategic Implications for Cutoff Modeling & CETCompass AI

1. **Independent Measurement of Approved vs Total Shifts**:
   In `Institute_Intake_Summary`, changes are now reported in three distinct blocks:
   - **Approved Changes** (`approved_change_24_to_25_abs`, etc.)
   - **TFWS Changes** (`tfws_change_24_to_25_abs`, etc.)
   - **Combined Total Changes** (`combined_total_change_24_to_25_abs`, etc.)
   This ensures complete transparency for colleges where approved seats remained static while TFWS seats adjusted.
2. **Leading Zero Integrity**:
   `institute_code` (e.g. `'01002'`) and `branch_code` (e.g. `'002'`) remain strings across all CSV and Excel files, guaranteeing zero mismatch during downstream joins with candidate preference lists and historical cutoff ranks.
